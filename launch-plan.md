# STRYV Launch Plan (Option A — Bootstrap)

You picked **Option A (~$200–450)**: launch lean on POD, prove demand with content. This is the
execution plan — exactly what to do, and the first 15 videos to make. The budget buys tools,
samples, and seeding; **your effort buys the content**, which is what actually validates STRYV.

---

## Part 1 — Get live (the checklist)

**Week 1 — setup**
- [ ] **Verify STRYV** — `.com`, USPTO apparel search, `@stryv` on IG + TikTok (do this first;
      handle availability can force a name tweak).
- [ ] **Finalize print-ready art** — light/reversed version for dark shirts + vectorize the
      wordmark (see [`brand-identity.md`](./brand-identity.md)).
- [ ] **Pick provider + blank** — Tapstitch or Apliiq on a heavyweight (280–400 GSM) blank
      (see [`sourcing-and-ai-design.md`](./sourcing-and-ai-design.md)).
- [ ] **Set up the store** — TikTok Shop (free) or Shopify Basic. One hero product page to start.
- [ ] **Order samples to yourself** (~$60–120) — the oversized tee + one colorway. Non-negotiable.
- [ ] **Set up socials** — `@stryv` on TikTok + IG. Bio: what it is + who it's for + link.

**Week 2 — while samples ship**
- [ ] **Batch-film your first 10–15 videos** (list below) using the sample + b-roll.
- [ ] **Build a shortlist of 10–15 micro gym creators** to gift product to (seeding).
- [ ] Write product copy + shipping/return policy (honest shipping times).

**Launch + ongoing**
- [ ] Go live. Post **daily**. Seed product to creators. Engage every comment.
- [ ] Judge designs on **30–50 videos**, not 3. Iterate the ones that get traction.

> Total out of pocket to get here: **~$200–450.** The rest is reps.

---

## Part 2 — Your first 15 videos

Format rules for every one: **hook in the first 2 seconds**, vertical, trending audio, on-screen
text, and a soft CTA ("link in bio"). Batch-film several in one session to stay consistent.

1. **The pump-cover reveal** — baggy STRYV tee pre-workout → train → remove for the pump. *The*
   native format for your product. Make several versions.
2. **"Why heavyweight hits different"** — hold up the 400 GSM tee, show the drape/weight vs a thin
   cheap tee. Sell the fabric.
3. **First sample unboxing** — founder POV opening your own STRYV package. Authentic origin energy.
4. **"I started a gym brand"** — build-in-public origin story. People root for the founder.
5. **Fit check** — how the oversized cut sits; show it on different builds if you can.
6. **Gym-to-street styling** — wear it lifting, then out. Sells the athleisure crossover.
7. **The story behind the red V** — the design meaning. Builds brand identity.
8. **"POV: an oversized gym tee that actually fits right"** — relatable pain point → solution.
9. **Day-in-the-life training in STRYV** — a full session, product on the whole time.
10. **Cheap tee vs STRYV** — side-by-side quality comparison. Justifies the price.
11. **Motivational + lifting b-roll** — heavy sets, on-screen "KEEP STRYVING." Shareable.
12. **Drop teaser / countdown** — build urgency before launch and each restock.
13. **UGC repost** — re-share a creator/customer wearing it (once seeding lands).
14. **"3 ways to style an oversized gym tee"** — practical, saveable, broad reach.
15. **Founder talking-head: the mission** — why you built STRYV, what the club stands for.

### Content principles
- **The product is on-screen in every video** — you're marketing STRYV, not making random gym content.
- **Post daily; batch-film weekly.** Consistency beats production quality early.
- **Double down on winners.** When one video pops, make 5 variations of it immediately.
- **Seeding > ads at this stage.** Authentic creators in-the-gym outperform polished ads.

---

## What success looks like in the first 30 days

1. Are videos getting **views**? (No → content/hook problem.)
2. Are viewers **clicking to the product**? (No → hook or offer.)
3. Are clickers **buying**? (Traffic but no sales → price, trust, or product page.)

Each answer points to one specific fix. Don't overhaul everything — change one variable at a time.
Expect the real signal around **30–50 videos / 60–90 days**. Persistence past the awkward early
phase is the whole game.
