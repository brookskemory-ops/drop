# Finding a Cheap-but-Quality Manufacturer

The honest framing: **"cheapest per unit" and "cheapest to start" are opposite ends.** The
lowest per-unit price needs bulk overseas manufacturing (upfront cash + MOQ + vetting risk); the
lowest risk is POD (highest per-unit cost). Since **STRYV isn't validated yet** (you have designs,
not proven sellers), match the tier to your stage — don't over-commit before demand is real.

---

## The three tiers

| Tier | Per-unit (heavyweight tee) | Upfront / MOQ | Custom neck label? | Best stage |
|------|---------------------------|---------------|--------------------|-----------|
| **1. POD** | ~$15–25 landed | $0, no MOQ | Some providers (Apliiq) | Launch & test designs **now** |
| **2. Blanks + printing** | ~$8–14 | Buy 24–50+ blanks | Only if you relabel | After a design shows early traction |
| **3. Custom cut-&-sew (OEM)** | **$8–20** @ ~50 → **$8–12** @ 500+ | $$$ + MOQ 30–50+ + samples | **Yes, fully yours** | Proven winners; best margin |

> A basic blank tee is ~$2–5; a fully custom heavyweight streetwear tee is ~$8–20 at low MOQ,
> dropping ~50–60% toward $8–12 once you're ordering 500+.

---

## Tier 1 — POD (start here)

No MOQ, zero inventory risk, highest per-unit. Use it to launch STRYV and find out which designs
actually sell before spending on bulk. Providers: **Tapstitch** (premium, up to 430 GSM),
**Apliiq** (streetwear blanks + custom neck labels), Printful/Gelato. See
[`sourcing-and-ai-design.md`](./sourcing-and-ai-design.md).

## Tier 2 — Premium blanks + printing (the smart middle path)

Buy **known-good premium blanks wholesale** and print them (local screen printer or DTF). You
lock in blank quality and improve margin vs POD, without cut-and-sew MOQ/risk.

- **Blank brands (all reliable, ~$3–8/blank):** **Bella+Canvas** (LA, Airlume combed cotton,
  prints beautifully), **AS Colour** (streetwear staple, heavyweight options), **Cotton Heritage**
  (mill-direct, WRAP-certified, fast US warehouses), Stanley/Stella (EU/organic).
- **Catch:** the **neck label shows the blank brand** unless you remove/relabel — fine for
  launching, less "fully ours" premium than custom.

## Tier 3 — Custom cut-and-sew (once validated)

Your own garment, your own labels, cheapest per unit at volume, best margins. This is where a
real brand ends up — after designs are proven.

**Where cheap + quality actually coexist:** **Pakistan (Sialkot)** and **China** manufacturing
hubs give export-quality heavyweight (up to ~400+ GSM) at a fraction of US cost, with custom neck
labels/tags. Candidates surfaced in research (claiming low MOQ ~20–50 pcs) — **vet before
trusting**: Nofal Apparel, IK Industries (Sialkot, PK); Clothing Manufacturer Ltd. (CN);
FlorixSports; AKCN. Plus **Alibaba verified suppliers** with **Trade Assurance** (escrow).

> ⚠️ No specific factory here is personally verified — these came from search/marketing pages.
> Quality and communication reliability **vary a lot**. The vetting below is what turns "cheap"
> into "cheap *and* quality."

---

## How to vet a manufacturer (this is the "quality" part — don't skip)

1. **Always order a sample first.** Pay for a sample run before any bulk order. Wash it, stretch
   it, wear it. No sample = no order, ever.
2. **Use escrow/Trade Assurance.** On Alibaba, pay through Trade Assurance so funds are protected.
   Never wire money to an unverified supplier's personal account.
3. **Check certifications.** WRAP (ethical production), OEKO-TEX (safe fabric/dyes), BSCI. Real
   factories have them; ask for the certificates.
4. **Start small.** First order at the MOQ minimum, not a huge run — even after a good sample.
5. **Test communication.** Fast, clear, English-fluent replies and willingness to share factory
   photos/videos = good sign. Slow, evasive, or pushy = walk away.
6. **Read reviews / order history.** On Alibaba, prefer suppliers with years of history, high
   response rate, and real transaction volume.
7. **Confirm the specs in writing:** GSM, fabric composition, fit/measurements, stitching,
   label placement, colors (get a physical color match). Put it all in the tech pack.

### Red flags
- Price dramatically below everyone else (corners get cut on fabric/labor).
- No samples offered, or refuses to do a small first run.
- Requests full payment upfront to a personal account, off-platform.
- Vague answers on fabric weight/composition or certifications.

---

## Recommended sequence for STRYV

1. **Now:** Launch on **POD** (Tier 1) — validate which STRYV designs sell. Near-zero risk.
2. **Early traction:** Optionally move a winner to **premium blanks + printing** (Tier 2) for
   better margin while volume is still small.
3. **Proven sellers:** Move to **custom cut-and-sew** (Tier 3) at low MOQ (~50) with a vetted
   Pakistan/China factory — custom labels, heavyweight fabric, real margins. Sample hard first.

**The mistake to avoid:** ordering a bulk cut-and-sew run of an unproven design to "save money."
A dud run is how new brands burn their whole budget. Prove demand cheap, *then* scale to bulk.
