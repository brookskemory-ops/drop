# STRYV Signature Motif — ACHILLES

The staple mascot for STRYV: **Achilles**, with the **heel/boot** as the recurring icon. This
is the backbone of the brand's visual identity and its story. Public-domain mythology → no IP risk.

---

## Why it works (the story = your moat)

The **"Achilles heel"** is the most famous weakness in history. A gym brand exists to **eliminate
weak points.** So STRYV flips it:

> Everyone has an Achilles heel. STRYV is about **forging yours into strength.**

That's an ethos, not just a logo — the thing cult brands (Darc's "Wolves") have and knockoffs
don't. It gives every product, caption, and drop a meaning to rally around.

**The signature detail:** put the **blood-red accent at the heel.** Your red "V" → becomes the
red at Achilles' heel → the weakness you forge. One loaded color across the whole brand.

## Neck label / hangtag idea
- Woven label: the **heel/greave (or helmet) icon** + "STRYV ATHLETIC CLUB" + a tiny red heel dot.
- Hangtag inside: the story — *"Everyone has an Achilles heel. Forge yours."* Turns a tag into brand.

## Print notes
- **Statue / warrior / heel-photographic concepts** → print as full-image **DTF** (they won't
  vectorize cleanly; design with intentional dark, faded edges so they sit well on black tees).
- **Icon / wordmark / crest (graphic)** → extract clean via the `art/` pipeline (transparent +
  reversed-for-dark-shirt versions).
- Keep the palette **black + bone-white + one blood-red** — that restraint is the premium signal.
- The **red always at the heel** — make that your consistency rule across every design.
