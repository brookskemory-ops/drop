## The 60-second version

Dropshipping = you sell a product, and a supplier ships it directly to your customer.
You never hold inventory. Your real job is **marketing and customer trust**, not logistics.

Three things separate people who make money from people who lose it:

1. **Pick a product that solves a real problem** — not just something that looks viral.
   Margins matter: aim for a **3x+ markup** (sell at $30–45 what costs you $8–12).
2. **Make good video content.** In 2026, cheap paid traffic is gone. Organic TikTok /
   Reels content *is* the business. Plan to post 30–50 videos before judging a product.
3. **Give it time.** Realistic timeline is **60–90 days** to consistent revenue. Your
   first month is tuition — you're testing products and learning the algorithm.

> ⚠️ This is a real business, not passive income. Most stores that fail do so because
> the owner quit after one product or expected week-one profits. Budget money you can
> afford to lose and treat the first few products as paid experiments.
