# STRYV Startup Budget — What It Actually Costs

Real numbers to launch STRYV, tuned to the POD-first plan. **The big advantage of POD: you don't
pay for a garment until a customer orders it** — so your startup cost is mostly tools, samples,
and marketing, *not* inventory. That's why you can genuinely start for a few hundred dollars.

Three ways to start, depending on budget:

---

## Option A — Bootstrap (~$200–450) · launch this month

Everything you need to be live and selling on POD. Organic content only.

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Store | $0–29 | TikTok Shop (free) or Shopify Basic (~$29/mo; often $1 first month) |
| Domain | ~$12/yr | Only if using Shopify |
| AI design tools | ~$20–40 | Midjourney (~$10/mo) + Kittl (~$12/mo); Ideogram has a free tier |
| **Samples to yourself** | ~$60–120 | 2–3 STRYV pieces — **non-negotiable**, you must hold them |
| Content gear | $0 | Your phone + free CapCut |
| Creator seeding | ~$50–100 | Gift product to a few micro gym creators |
| **Total** | **~$200–450** | Live, selling, testing designs |

## Option B — Recommended (~$700–1,500) · real momentum

Adds legal protection and enough seeding/ads to actually generate first sales data.

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Store (3 months) | ~$90 | Shopify Basic to get going |
| Domain | ~$12 | |
| AI design tools (2–3 mo) | ~$60–100 | Midjourney + Kittl while you build the line |
| Samples (few designs/colorways) | ~$150 | Incl. a dark colorway with the light logo version |
| **Trademark (STRYV)** | ~$250–350 | USPTO filing, 1 class (apparel). Optional but smart before you build equity |
| Content props/lighting | ~$50–100 | Simple setup for better video |
| Creator seeding | ~$200–400 | Gift to 10–15 micro-influencers — this drives your launch |
| Small paid ad test | ~$100–200 | Only to amplify organic videos that already work |
| **Total** | **~$700–1,500** | Protected brand + real marketing push |

## Option C — Comfortable (~$2,500–5,000+) · owned inventory + polish

Once designs are proven (or if you want to launch premium from day one). Adds a bulk run and pro assets.

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Everything in Option B | ~$1,000 | |
| First custom bulk run | ~$600–1,800 | 50 units/style @ ~$12 (see [`finding-a-manufacturer.md`](./finding-a-manufacturer.md)) |
| Pro photoshoot | ~$300–800 | Apparel-literate photographer |
| Branded packaging | ~$100–300 | Mailers, tissue, stickers, woven labels ($1–3/unit) |
| Bigger ad budget | ~$300–500+ | |
| LLC registration | ~$50–500 | Varies by state |
| **Total** | **~$2,500–5,000+** | Full brand, owned stock, best margins |

---

## Don't forget ongoing monthly costs

- Shopify: ~$29/mo (skip if TikTok-Shop-only)
- Design tools: ~$20–30/mo (can cancel once designs are set)
- Ad spend: whatever you choose to scale with

## Where the money should go (this matters more than the total)

> The founders who fail aren't the ones who spent too much on product — they're the ones who
> spent **everything** on product and had nothing left for content, seeding, and marketing.

Weight your budget toward **getting STRYV seen**, not toward inventory. On POD, product is
covered by the sale — so your dollars should buy samples, creator seeding, and content, which is
what actually validates designs and drives sales.

## My recommendation for you

**Start with Option A (~$200–450).** You have the brand, designs, and a printer — the only thing
left to prove is whether people buy. Launch lean on POD, put your energy into 30–50 videos and
creator seeding, and let real sales tell you which designs work. **Reinvest profit** into Option
B/C (trademark, then a bulk run of your proven winners). Don't spend $3k up front to find out
whether design #1 sells — let the cheap test answer that first.

**Bottom line:** you can genuinely launch STRYV for **under $500**. The real investment isn't
money — it's the 60–90 days of consistent content it takes to get traction.
