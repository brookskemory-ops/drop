# Budget & Tools — What This Actually Costs

Dropshipping is low-inventory, not zero-cost. Here's a realistic view so you don't get
surprised. Budget money you can afford to lose — treat early products as paid experiments.

## Realistic startup budget

| Item | Lean start | Comfortable start |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| Store platform (TikTok Shop / Shopify Starter) | $0–5/mo | $29+/mo (Shopify Basic) |
| Domain (optional early) | $0 | ~$12/yr |
| Sample product (order to yourself) | $10–40 | $10–40 |
| Content: phone + free editing | $0 | small props/lighting ($30–80) |
| Initial ad testing (optional, later) | $0 | $100–300 |
| Product research tool (optional) | $0 | $40–100/mo |
| **Realistic first-month total** | **~$30–80** | **~$200–500** |

You can genuinely start for **under $100** if you lead with organic content and only order a
sample. The bigger numbers come later, *after* you've proven a product converts.

> The real "cost" is time and persistence: 30–50 videos and 60–90 days before consistent
> revenue is the normal timeline. Plan for it.

## Tool stack

**Store / selling**
- TikTok Shop — in-app checkout where the attention is (needs business docs to get approved).
- Shopify — full control, multi-channel; Starter plan built for social selling.

**Suppliers / fulfillment**
- AliExpress — widest selection, slower shipping.
- CJdropshipping, Zendrop, Spocket, Syncee, Doba — better tooling, some with US/EU warehouses
  for faster delivery (a real trust advantage).

**Content & creative**
- Your phone (best camera you have) + CapCut or your phone's native editor — free and enough.
- Canva — logo, product-image touch-ups, simple branding.
- TikTok Creative Center — research trending formats and top-performing ads (free).

**Product research (optional, paid)**
- Minea, WinningHunter, Sell The Trend, Dropship.io — surface products currently spending on ads.
- Google Trends — free, and enough to check whether demand is rising or dead.

## Where beginners waste money

- **Premium themes / apps before a single sale.** A clean free setup converts fine.
- **Paid ads before organic validation.** Prove it converts with cheap/free content first.
- **Buying inventory.** The whole point is you don't. Only order samples.
- **Expensive courses.** The free guides here + actually shipping 30 videos teach you more.

## Don't-skip legal/admin basics

- Register a business and business bank account (also required for TikTok Shop approval).
- Set aside for taxes on profit from day one.
- Publish clear, honest **shipping-time and refund policies** — vagueness here is the top
  cause of disputes and chargebacks.
- For electrical/health-adjacent products, source from suppliers with proper certification
  (CE/FCC/UL) and never make medical claims.
