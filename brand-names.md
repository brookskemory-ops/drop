# Brand Name Ideas — Men's Gym Basics

Curated name concepts grouped by the four aesthetics in
[`design-prompts.md`](./design-prompts.md), so the name matches the look. ⭐ = strong picks.

> **Before you commit to ANY name, check all four:**
> 1. **`.com` domain** available (buyable, not $5k premium)
> 2. **Trademark** — search USPTO (and your country) for the same/similar name *in apparel*
> 3. **Handles** — `@name` free on Instagram **and** TikTok (this is where you'll live)
> 4. **Shopify** store URL
>
> Coined/invented words (below) are far more likely to clear all four than common words.
> Also: **don't** pick anything close to an existing gym brand — legal risk + customer confusion.

## 1. Minimalist Heavyweight (premium, clean, "quiet luxury")

Short, refined, looks premium as a plain wordmark.

- ⭐ **HEWN** — "carved from stone"; perfect for heavyweight/strength
- ⭐ **KORE** — core, clean, ownable spelling
- **FORMA** — form/physique, premium European feel
- **STOIC** — discipline/lifting mindset (check TM — popular concept)
- **ANVL** — anvil, stripped vowels = modern + more available
- **HEFT** — literally "heaviness"; blunt and memorable
- **GRANITE** — solid, heavy, premium
- **TENET** — principle/discipline; clean one-word

## 2. Bold Typographic (loud, hype, pump-cover culture)

Punchy, aggressive, great as big back typography.

- ⭐ **BRUTE** — short, heavy, unforgettable
- ⭐ **FERAL** — primal, edgy, ownable
- **PRIMAL** — instinctive strength
- **HAVOC** — aggressive, memorable
- **VANDAL** — rebellious streetwear energy
- **SURGE** — energy/pump
- **NOCTURNE** — "train after dark" angle, distinctive
- **RECKLESS** — bold attitude wordmark

## 3. Retro / 90s Vintage Athletic (collegiate, warm, nostalgic)

Leans into the 90s-revival trend; works with varsity/badge treatments.

- ⭐ **GOLDEN ERA** — nods to classic Muscle-Beach lifting culture
- ⭐ **DIVISION** — "Division I" athletic feel
- **HERITAGE ATHLETIC** — classic, trustworthy
- **THE YARD** — gritty-but-collegiate, great on a back print
- **REP CLUB** — "reps" + club membership vibe
- **FIELDHOUSE** — old-school athletic
- **LETTERMAN** — varsity nostalgia
- **CIRCA '92** — instant retro anchor (pick your year)

## 4. Gritty / Grunge Hardcore (dark, underground, serious lifters)

For the hardcore-lifting aesthetic; high contrast, distressed.

- ⭐ **IRONCLAD** — strength + durability in one word
- ⭐ **FORGE** (or **FORGED**) — made/hardened by fire; strong verb energy
- **GRIT** — short, punchy, on-message
- **DUNGEON** — "dungeon gym" hardcore-lifter reference (they love it)
- **CHALK** — lifting chalk; minimal and insider
- **CALLOUS** — earned-through-work imagery
- **FURNACE** — heat/intensity
- **BRUTALIST** — heavy, architectural, distinctive

## Coined / invented wildcards (best availability + trademarkable)

These aren't dictionary words, so `.com` + handles + trademark are much likelier to be open —
and they read premium. Aesthetic-flexible.

- ⭐ **STRYV** — from "strive," modern stripped spelling
- ⭐ **KRAVN** — from "craving/crave"; hard, brandable
- **ATLYS** — atlas/strength, premium feel
- **RONIN** — masterless samurai; discipline/lone-wolf (check TM — used elsewhere)
- **VÆRK** — Scandinavian "work"; distinctive, premium
- **NOKTA** — invented, sleek, ownable
- **KAEL** — short, strong, brandable
- **HÆVN** — "haven"/heaven stylized; edgy premium

## Naming principles (why these work)

- **Short: 1–2 syllables.** Easy to say, spell, and set as a clean wordmark/logo.
- **Ownable, not descriptive.** "BRUTE" beats "Best Gym Tees" — you can trademark it and rank for it.
- **Room to grow.** Avoid boxing yourself into only tees (e.g. names that aren't "…Shirts Co")
  so you can expand into full apparel/lifestyle later.
- **Say it out loud.** If it's awkward to say in a video or hard to hear, cut it.
- **Check the handle first.** A perfect name with no free `@handle` on IG/TikTok isn't usable —
  handle availability often decides the winner.
