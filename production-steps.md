# Getting the Shirts Made — Step by Step

The exact path from your AI mockups to a physical STRYV sample in your hands. Do these in order.
The real first blocker is **file prep** — your current designs are AI images, not print files.

---

## Step 0 — Make print-ready files (the actual first task)

You need, for **each** design (front wordmark + back graphic):
- A **transparent-background PNG at 300 DPI**
- A **dark-background version** (white + red art) so it shows on black/charcoal shirts
- Correct size: **back ~3600–4200px wide** (~12–14"); **front chest small** (~3–4")

**Easiest free way — Photopea (photopea.com, no install):**
1. Open your design PNG in Photopea.
2. **Remove the white background:** Select → Magic Cut / or use the Magic Wand on the white area
   → Delete. You'll see a checkerboard (transparency).
3. Clean stray edges; make sure only the logo remains.
4. **Export:** File → Export as → **PNG**, at the target pixel width above.
5. **Make the white version:** duplicate the layer → lock transparency → fill the black parts
   with white (keep the red red) → export as a second PNG. This is your dark-shirt version.

*(Kittl does the same and can also **vectorize** the wordmark so it's razor-sharp at any size —
worth it for the logo you'll reuse on every product and the neck label.)*

## Step 2 — Create account + connect your store

1. Sign up (free).
2. Connect it to your store: **TikTok Shop** (free) or **Shopify Basic** (~$29/mo). For content-led
   selling, TikTok Shop is the lowest-friction start.

## Step 3 — Choose the blank

- Product: **heavyweight oversized tee** (your hero / pump-cover piece).
- Fabric: **280–400+ GSM** — pick the heaviest premium option (this is your quality signal).
- Start with **1–2 core colors** (e.g. black + off-white). Don't over-range on day one.

## Step 4 — Upload the art + set placements

1. Add the product, open the design editor.
2. **Back placement:** upload the large back graphic; center it, size to ~12–14" wide.
3. **Front placement:** upload the small chest mark; position **left chest** (~3–4"), or small
   centered. Each placement is a separate upload.
4. Use the **white/reversed** files on dark garments, the **black** files on light garments.
5. Preview the mockup; nudge placement/size until it looks right.

## Step 5 — Order a sample to yourself

**This is non-negotiable — order before you ever sell.**
1. Order **one of each** (e.g. the tee in black *and* off-white).
2. Ship to yourself. Expect a few days to ~2 weeks depending on provider/warehouse.
3. Cost: ~$60–120 total (already in your Option A budget).

## Step 6 — Inspect the sample (checklist)

- [ ] **Print quality:** crisp edges, no cracking/fading, colors match (esp. the red).
- [ ] **Placement:** front and back sit where you designed them; not crooked or too high/low.
- [ ] **Fabric feel:** genuinely heavyweight and premium — does it justify the price?
- [ ] **Fit:** the oversized cut sits right; check the size chart matches reality.
- [ ] **Wash test:** wash + dry once; confirm the print survives and fabric holds up.

If anything's off, adjust the file/placement or try the other print method (DTG vs DTF) and
re-sample. **Don't launch on a sample you're not proud to wear.**

## Step 7 — Publish + prep to launch

1. Once the sample passes, finalize the product page: photos of the real sample, honest sizing,
   clear shipping times, price (hold **4x+ landed cost**).
2. You're ready for the [`launch-plan.md`](./launch-plan.md) content push.
